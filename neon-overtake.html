Neon Overtake

A fast-paced, neon-styled 2D highway driving game developed in Python for Windows. Switch lanes, dodge unpredictable traffic, and overtake as many vehicles as possible. The faster you progress, the more challenging the road becomes.

02 / About the game

Simple to learn. Hard to master.

An arcade experience built around quick decisions, replayability, and beating your own record.

Game features

  • Vertical highway driving with lane-based movement
  • Random traffic positions for replayable runs
  • Increasing difficulty as your overtakes accumulate
  • Score and personal-best tracking
  • Main menu, audio settings, and retry screen

How to play

  • ← / A — move left
  • → / D — move right
  • Esc — pause the game
  • R or Space — retry after a crash
  • Overtake traffic without colliding to increase your score.
